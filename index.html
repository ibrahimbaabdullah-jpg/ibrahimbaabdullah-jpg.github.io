Biography

I am a nursing professional and health informatics researcher with experience in clinical care, electronic health records, quantitative research, and digital health projects.

I hold a Master of Advanced Practice Nursing from Queensland University of Technology (QUT), where my dissertation was awarded Distinction, and a Bachelor of Nursing from the University of Newcastle, Australia. Master’s research

Nursing Informatics Competence and Attitudes Towards Computers Among Final-Year Baccalaureate Nursing Students at Najran University: A Cross-Sectional Study in Saudi Arabia.

This quantitative study examined self-reported nursing informatics competence and attitudes towards computer use among final-year nursing students. I designed and administered a 60-item bilingual Qualtrics survey, managed responses from 48 students, and performed data cleaning, coding, scoring, and statistical analyses using IBM SPSS.

The study used Benner’s Novice to Expert model as its primary framework. The research included protocol development, literature review, questionnaire permissions, ethics approvals at QUT and Najran University, and coordination of data collection. The dissertation was awarded Distinction, and an evidence-based report was prepared for Najran University.

Queensland University of Technology, July 2023–November 2025.
Supervisor: Dr Aaron Conway.
